# BCC homepage mockup v1: photo and video brief

Mockup: `mockup/index.html` (desktop, 1440 px design width).
Every placeholder in the mockup has an ID (V1, P1–P7). The ID is printed on the placeholder and used in this brief.

## General rules for all shots
- Shoot on a bright day. Switch on all interior lights and open the blinds.
- Tidy every space first: no cables, bags, paper piles or personal items.
- Shoot in colour at the highest resolution available. Shoot RAW if you can, or high-quality JPEG.
- Keep the camera level so vertical lines stay straight. Shoot at chest height (about 1.2–1.4 m).
- For anyone who can be recognised, get a signed image-release form.
- Take 3–5 variations of each shot, including one slightly wider than you think you need, so the crop can be adjusted later.

**Shooting notes**
- **P1 Coworking Area:** stand in a corner so the room recedes diagonally, with desks, chairs and windows in frame. One or two people working, seen from behind or the side, make it feel lived-in; this is optional. Shoot the vertical frame directly rather than cropping from a horizontal photo.
- **P2 Private Office:** shoot from the doorway into the office, showing the desk, chair and window. No people. Leave the door frame just out of shot.
- **P3 Building Exterior:** the facade at 77, route d'Arlon, with BCC signage visible if there is any. Stand across the street. Shoot horizontally and leave extra sky and ground, because the image is cropped to a very wide strip; keep the important part in the middle third. Overcast daylight or the 20 minutes after sunset (lights on inside) both work well. Avoid parked cars in front of the entrance if possible.
- **P4 Main Entrance:** the front door and entrance area with BCC signage, taken from about 3–4 m away. Vertical. No people, or one person entering, seen from behind.
- **P5–P7 Portraits:** optional. Use them only if the clients agree and can supply or sit for a photo. Plain or blurred background, natural light, looking at the camera. If portraits aren't possible, the cards work without them and the circles are removed.

**Real BCC photos already used (no reshoot needed):** meeting room, reception hall (Bloc C), entrance with glazed reception, and the lounge/meeting room with table football. These appear in "OUR SPACES".

**Live-site images not used:** the Kirchberg staircase, the hand holding a plant, and the robotic hand. These are stock photos that don't show BCC, so the mockup replaces them with P3/P4 and the hero video. They can be put back if you prefer.

## B. Video request list

### V1: Hero walkthrough loop (recommended)
- **Where it is used:** the large frame directly under "Business Center Capellen" at the top of the homepage. It plays muted, automatically, on a loop. This is the single most important asset for the premium feel.
- **Location:** outside the building → through the main entrance → reception hall → corridor → coworking area or meeting room.
- **What appears:** the building and the BCC entrance, the reception, bright workspaces. One or two people at work in the background are good (with releases). No close-up faces, no screens showing private information.
- **Camera movement:** slow, smooth forward walk on a gimbal or phone stabiliser at walking pace or slower. Inside rooms, one slow sideways slide (left to right) of 2–3 m. No fast pans, no zooming.
- **Format:** horizontal 16:9, 4K (3840 × 2160) if possible, otherwise 1080p. 25 or 30 fps, or 50/60 fps for smoother slow motion. The site crops it to a 2:1 strip, so keep important content in the middle band of the frame. The lower-right corner sits under the "Work with us" badge, so avoid important detail there.
- **Duration:** a 15–20 second final edit. Record either one continuous 40–60 second walk, or 4 separate clips of 8–10 seconds each:
  1. exterior approach to the entrance
  2. the door opening or walking through the entrance
  3. reception hall
  4. slow slide across the coworking area or meeting room
- **Sound:** not needed (the video plays muted).
- **Also needed:** one still frame (poster image) of the best moment, shown while the video loads.

No other video is recommended for v1. Every other image slot is a photo.
