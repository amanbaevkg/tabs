# BCC homepage concept v2: changes, reasons and asset brief

Mockup: `mockup-v2/index.html` (desktop, 1440 px), now revision v2.1.

**v2.1 changes:** softer connected palette (warm off-white, sand, stone, grey-blue haze, slate navy; no black, no orange); hero rebuilt around the full-width video with a centred title at 72 px instead of 226 px; lighter header (logo left, thin nav, text "Contact" link, hides on scroll down and returns on scroll up). Everything else unchanged. Concept v1 in `mockup/` is kept for comparison.
Content: every heading, paragraph, testimonial, name, role, link and contact detail is copied from businesscentercapellen.lu.
Reference: Mindspace (mindspace.me) was studied from desktop screenshots for its level of craft. No text, colour, component or layout was copied from it.

## 2. Why each major section changed

- **Colour:** navy and stone read as serious, calm and architectural. Orange read as promotional, so it's gone. Cyan survives as a thread back to the BCC logo.
- **Typography:** wide, light letterforms feel like building signage and give the page presence without shouting. The single italic serif word in each big statement adds an editorial, hospitality tone.
- **Hero:** the first screen now has to show the building and its spaces in motion. A video with oversized type over it does that immediately. The overlapping entrance photo pulls the eye down into the page.
- **Offers:** the two office types are what BCC sells, so they get the largest images on the page. Mirrored compositions (light chapter, then navy chapter) make them feel like two distinct products rather than two list items.
- **They trust us:** placing the logos inside the navy chapter makes them part of the story rather than a strip in between. The white logo artwork also sits correctly on dark.
- **Building tomorrow's now:** this headline is the most "statement" text BCC has, so it gets a full-screen architectural treatment.
- **Our Spaces:** a horizontal sequence feels like walking through the building and scales to more photos later without redesigning.
- **Testimonials:** they are strong, specific texts. An editorial layout lets them be read like quotes in a magazine, and removes the placeholder avatars.
- **Language selector:** small text codes with a thin cyan underline stay quiet in the header and give all four languages equal weight. Clicking FR, DE or LU marks it active; the content stays in English for now. Russian can be added as a fifth code later without changing the layout.

## 4. Videos to record

### V1: Building Entrance → Workspace (hero background)
- **Where it plays:** full-screen behind "Business Center Capellen", muted, autoplay loop.
- **Route:** approach the building from outside, then the main entrance door, the reception hall, the corridor, and finally the coworking area or a meeting room.
- **What appears:** facade and entrance, reception, bright workspaces. One or two people working in the background are fine (with releases); no close-up faces, no readable screens or documents.
- **Camera:** gimbal or stabilised phone, slow steady forward walk (slower than normal walking). In the final room, one slow sideways slide of 2–3 m. No pans, no zoom, no handheld shake.
- **Format:** horizontal 16:9, 4K (3840 × 2160) preferred, 25 or 50 fps. No sound needed.
- **Framing (v2.1 hero):** the title now sits in the centre of the frame, so keep the centre fairly calm (walls, floor, soft light) and let the detail and movement happen in the outer thirds. Avoid bright windows directly in the centre. The lower-right corner sits under the overlapping entrance photo.
- **Length:** final edit 20–25 s. Record either one continuous walk of 60–90 s, or 5 clips of 8–10 s each: exterior approach · door · reception · corridor · workspace slide.
- **Also needed:** one sharp still frame from the best moment (poster image shown while loading).

No other video is needed for v2. P4 or P8 could later become short (8–10 s) slow-motion loops, filmed from a tripod with no camera movement, if you want more motion.
