## Required videos (1)

**R-V1: Hero walkthrough, "Building Entrance → Workspace"**
- **Where:** full-screen background of the hero, behind the title and the workspace selector. It plays muted, automatically, on a loop.
- **What:** approach the building, then the entrance door, the reception hall, the corridor, and a furnished office or co-working space.
- **Orientation · ratio:** horizontal 16:9. 4K (3840×2160) preferred, otherwise 1080p. 25 or 50 fps.
- **Duration:** final loop 20–25 s. Record one continuous walk of 60–90 s, or 5 clips of 8–10 s each (exterior · door · reception · corridor · workspace).
- **Camera movement:** slow, steady forward walk on a gimbal or stabilised phone, slower than normal walking pace. Finish with one slow sideways slide (2–3 m) in the workspace. No pans, no zoom, no handheld shake.
- **Framing:** the title and selector sit in the centre, so keep the centre calm (walls, floor, soft light) and let the detail be in the outer thirds. Avoid bright windows in the middle of the frame.
- **People:** optional, 1–2 in the background, no close-up faces, with signed image releases. No readable screens or documents.
- **Sound:** not needed.

## General shooting rules
- Shoot on a bright day, all lights on, blinds open, spaces tidied (no cables, packaging or boards).
- Keep the camera level so vertical lines stay straight; shoot from chest height.
- Take 3–5 variations of each shot, including one slightly wider than needed.
- Use no filters or heavy editing, so all photos match.
- Get a signed image release for anyone who can be recognised.
